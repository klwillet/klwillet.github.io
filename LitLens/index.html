Key Features

Smart Annotation Tools

Highlight text, add margin notes, and insert comments directly into passages. Annotations stay anchored to the text, making it easy to revisit ideas, quotes, and questions while reading.

Theme & Literary Device Tracker

Tag passages with custom labels like symbolism, motif, imagery, irony, or character development. The app automatically organizes these into visual collections so patterns and themes become easy to spot.

Idea & Insight Board

All notes and annotations are pulled into a central dashboard where users can connect ideas across chapters, texts, or authors—perfect for essay planning, discussion prep, and literary analysis.
